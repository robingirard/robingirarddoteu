How the world's energy sources stacked up

No energy source has ever been replaced. Coal did not replace wood, oil did not replace coal, gas did not replace oil — each was added on top, and the ones below kept growing. What we call past energy transitions were additions. The one we have to make now, removing sources, has no precedent here.

Sources. Energy Institute, Statistical Review of World Energy 2026, and Vaclav Smil, Energy Transitions (2017) for 1800–1965, both via Our World in Data (CC BY 4.0). Final energy: IEA, Total Final Consumption by Source.

Projections. Years past the dashed line are our own extrapolation, applying the growth rates of the IEA World Energy Outlook 2025 Stated Policies Scenario from the last observed year. The IEA publishes no by-fuel value for 2026, 2027 or 2030 — those are ours, not theirs.

Health warning. Traditional biomass is held constant after 2015 by the upstream source. The Energy Institute and the IEA disagree on world totals by about 2 %, mostly on coal.
