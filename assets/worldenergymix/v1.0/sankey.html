Where the energy actually goes

From what we dig up, to what crosses the meter, to what a machine turns into something useful. The chain stops at useful energy: beyond it no efficiency is definable — a perfectly insulated building would need no heat at all, which would make its efficiency infinite.

Sources. Primary: Energy Institute, Statistical Review of World Energy 2026, via Our World in Data. Final: IEA, Total Final Consumption by Source. Device efficiencies: Cullen & Allwood (2010), Energy 35(5):2059–2069, table 3, which publishes the energy efficiency and the quality factor separately — which is what lets the same chain be drawn under either ruler.

What is estimated. How each carrier's energy divides between its devices is read off the volumes in their figure 2 (2005) and rescaled to our carrier totals. Their efficiencies are 1990 device data rescaled by the authors to 2005; apart from lighting, raised to 22 % for an LED-dominated stock, we have not updated them — so this is closer to a portrait of the 2005 device stock than of today's. Coolers exceed 100 % energy efficiency (they move heat rather than make it) and are capped for drawing.

Projections. Years past the last observed one are our own extrapolation on the IEA World Energy Outlook 2025 Stated Policies growth rates.
