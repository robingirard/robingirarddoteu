# Figures and code

Every figure in these slides is a claim, and a claim without its
computation cannot be checked. So here is the code — one figure at a
time, as each is opened.

By **[Robin Girard]({{site.url}}/)**, MINES Paris — PSL, Centre PERSEE.
The slides themselves are on the
[course page]({{site.url}}/IntroductionToEnergy.html).

Everything below lives in one repository: [bachelor_intro_to_energy_figures](https://git.persee.minesparis.psl.eu/energy-alternatives/bachelor_intro_to_energy_figures).
Code under the MIT licence, figures under CC BY 4.0. **No third-party
data is redistributed**: where a figure consumes a dataset, the
repository tells you how to fetch it from the source, it does not ship
it.

## The Carnot ceiling, and its three uses

Les trois planches des slides 11, 28 et 32 du déck 3 : la même borne thermodynamique lue dans les trois sens — chaleur vers mouvement (le moteur), mouvement vers chaleur (la pompe à chaleur), mouvement vers froid (le réfrigérateur). C'est délibérément la même figure à chaque fois : le propos est qu'il n'y a qu'une seule borne.

## Higher and lower heating value

Le schéma de principe de la slide 29 du déck 2. Ce que le PCS compte et que le PCI ne compte pas, c'est la chaleur latente de la vapeur d'eau produite par la combustion — d'où les chaudières à condensation qui affichent plus de 100 % de rendement sans violer le premier principe.

## How this inventory was made

Not from memory. The 287 images embedded in the three `.pptx` files
were matched by fingerprint against the 1 673 image files of the course
folder — exact hash, then a perceptual hash that survives PowerPoint's
recompression, then for SVG a match on text content. **A figure is here
only if it is really in a published slide.**

About 230 of those images matched nothing, and that is worth saying:
they are photographs, screenshots of papers, and diagrams drawn
directly in PowerPoint. For those there is no code to publish — none
exists.
