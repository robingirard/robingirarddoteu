# The Net Zero Game

A teaching model of France in 2050. You drive transport, building heating and
industry against emission targets and against the physical constraints that make
electrification a trade-off rather than a free win — the winter electricity peak,
and the biogas, biofuel and biomass the country can actually supply.

By **[Robin Girard]({{site.url}}/)**, MINES Paris — PSL.

**[Play the current version]({{site.url}}/assets/netzerogame/latest/index.html)** — v0.14.

It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can save a
scenario to a file and load it back on another machine.

## Why it is worth checking

Every formula is declared in a YAML file rather than buried in a spreadsheet cell,
and every assumption carries its value, its bounds, its provenance and its sources.
The engine that runs in the browser, the annex the application displays and a
Python checker are all compiled from those same files, and the build fails if the
two engines disagree — so a value shown and a value used cannot differ.

The application has a **Controversy** tab naming the assumptions that are argued
over, and a **Contribute** tab for telling us what is wrong. Both are the point:
if a number looks wrong to you, that is a contribution.

Source code, including the model and its sources:
[git.persee.minesparis.psl.eu/energy-alternatives/The-net-zero-game](https://git.persee.minesparis.psl.eu/energy-alternatives/The-net-zero-game)
